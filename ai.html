Where AI took part, and where it did not

Where AI tools took part in our books and on this site, and where a person did the work.

On this site

Mr. Madi, the librarian, does not exist. His pictures and short films were made with AI tools from our own description of him. So were the rooms you walk through. He does not chat: he asks set questions and chooses from a fixed list of our books. Nothing you tap there is sent to an AI service.

In the books

Cover art and many illustrations were made with AI image tools, then chosen, corrected and laid out by a person. Texts were drafted or checked with the help of AI tools and edited by a person. Page layouts, puzzles and calendars are built by our own programs and checked before printing.

When a book is published through Amazon, the use of AI-generated content is declared there, as Amazon asks.

The colored examples

When you press “See it colored” on a coloring page, you see one possible version. Some of those examples were colored with an AI image tool. The line drawing is the one printed in the book.

What AI does not do here

  • It does not read what you color or write
  • It does not decide what you are shown or what you pay
  • It does not stand in for reviews. There are no invented reviews on this site

Found something odd?

AI tools make mistakes and so do people. If you see one, please tell us.
