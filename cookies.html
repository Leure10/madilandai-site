No cookies. A little memory, in your own browser

This site sets no cookies. Here is the little that stays in your browser, and how to erase it.

Why there is no banner

A banner asks permission for things that are not needed for what you came to do. This site has no such things: no analytics, no advertising, nothing loaded from other companies. The fonts and the films come from this site itself.
