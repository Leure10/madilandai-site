A calm record of every session

Built around the real rhythm of a treatment room, for a private practice, a spa, or mobile bodywork.

The Client Session Notes table logs each client (initials for privacy), the body area worked, the technique or modality, the pressure, and quick follow-up notes. It works like a simple, at-a-glance SOAP log. The names in “See a filled week” are made up.
