A story on the left, its picture on the right

Best friends Bodie and Jodie are getting ready for the big night. They pick pumpkins at the patch (one is so big it won't budge), scoop out the slimy insides, and draw a silly face, a shy face, and a puppy face. Bodie becomes a round, friendly pumpkin, and Jodie becomes a witch whose hat keeps flopping over her eyes.

But on Halloween night, a wobbly little ghost floats down Maple Street, and the tall old house at the end of the street has a very creaky gate. With a deep breath and a friend's hand to hold, shy Bodie learns that brave knees can wobble, and that the spookiest things can turn out to be friendly.

Read together, then color the scene

Younger children listen and color; early readers can read the story on their own. The line art is simple, with big shapes for small hands. Not scary at all: no monsters, no frights, just cozy fall fun.

What it gently shows

  • Being brave when something seems scary, even with wobbly knees
  • Sharing with a little friend who lost his treats
  • Saying hello to a neighbor everyone thought was spooky
  • Friendship, teamwork, and a lot of giggles
