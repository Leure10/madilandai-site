Plan the open, trade the plan, review the day

A two-page spread for every trading day. The left page is your pre-market plan: gappers with gap %, float, pre-market volume, relative volume and catalyst, the economic calendar, the day’s rules, your if-then scenarios and a map of key levels.

The right page is the session: a 19-row trade log, the day’s result after fees and your results by time of day. The trades in “See a filled page” are the sample printed in the book.
