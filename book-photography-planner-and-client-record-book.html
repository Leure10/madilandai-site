One book for the minis and the weddings

A photographer’s week does not run on the hour. This planner has forty-eight fifteen-minute slots per day, 8:00 in the morning to 8:00 at night: a morning of mini sessions one after another, then a portrait session or a wedding blocked across as many rows as it takes.

Every week has its own column for the shoots still waiting to be edited and the galleries you owe, so a delivery date never hides behind a busy week. Undated, so it starts in any month. The names in “See it filled in” are made up.

Weeks
52 weekly spreads, 48 fifteen-minute slots per day, 8 AM to 8 PM
Every week
A column for edits and galleries due, a day total and a week total
Clients
130 records, A to Z: name, phone, session, deposit and the date the gallery was sent
Up front
Packages with length, number of images and price, plus deposit, cancellation terms and gallery turnaround
Money
Twelve monthly sections: sessions, deposits, balances, prints and add-ons, totals
Dates
Undated, start any week
Size
8.5 × 11 in, 122 pages, white paper
