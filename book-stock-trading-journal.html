Write the thesis, manage the trade

Built for swing traders who hold for days or weeks. Every trade starts with a written thesis and the condition that proves it wrong, then the market context: the index against its 50- and 200-day averages, relative strength and the next earnings date.

A management ledger records every add, trim and stop move, with the trail method, position size in percent, return, dividends and the borrow fee for shorts. You close each trade with one honest line: the thesis was right, wrong or mixed. The trades in “See a filled page” are the sample printed in the book.

Why paper, when your broker already keeps the numbers?

It does keep the numbers. This journal keeps what it can't: the plan before the entry, the rules you followed or broke, how you felt, and the lesson you carry into next week.

In every journal

  • A filled-in sample page
  • Your trading plan and risk rules
  • Mistake codes #1–12 and emotion codes E1–E8
  • An equity curve in R
