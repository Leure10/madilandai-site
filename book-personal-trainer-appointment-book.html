The hours other appointment books leave out

Most appointment books start at eight and stop at six, which are exactly the hours a personal trainer is least busy. This one runs from 6:00 in the morning to 8:00 at night, in fifty-six fifteen-minute slots per day, Monday through Sunday across two facing pages.

Book a 30-minute assessment, a 45-minute session and a full hour back to back and see exactly where the gap is. Undated, so it starts in January, in September, in any year. The names in “See it filled in” are made up.
