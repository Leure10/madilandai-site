A locked room that opens honestly

Thistlewick Hall, January 1927. Sir Ambrose Pell held the estate for forty years and kept its secrets longer. On the coldest night of the year he is found in the orangery, beneath the citrus trees his father planted. The door is bolted on the inside. Twelve people had cause. Eleven alibis hold.

Every grid hides a sentence in its leftover letters: something a witness saw, or a ledger recorded, or a frozen path failed to show. Four grids make one chapter, and each chapter clears exactly one suspect. The whole first chapter is here to solve.
