For the people who run a small medical office

It is made for the receptionist, the office manager and the provider who books their own day: forty-eight fifteen-minute slots per day, 8:00 in the morning to 8:00 at night, all seven days the same width.

A fifteen-minute follow-up, a thirty-minute new-patient visit and a forty-five-minute procedure fit where they belong. Undated, so it runs as the main book or as the backup at reception. The names in “See it filled in” are made up.

Only what scheduling needs

Nothing in this book asks for diagnoses, dates of birth or insurance numbers. The opening pages remind staff to keep clinical details in the chart and the book out of public view.
