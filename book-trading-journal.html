One journal, every market, every trade reviewed

Every card records the asset class, the account and the currency, strike, call or put and expiry when it's an option, your $ per point, pip or tick, costs such as commissions, swap and funding, and time in the trade. Risk is sized the same way on every market, so results compare in R.

Short on time? Three boxes on every card are framed in bold: Result in R, Followed my rules, and Mistake #. They take about twenty seconds and give you the numbers that matter at the weekly review. The trades in “See a filled page” are the sample printed in the book.

Why paper, when your broker already keeps the numbers?

It does keep the numbers. This journal keeps what it can't: the plan before the entry, the rules you followed or broke, how you felt, and the lesson you carry into next week.
