Track every pip, review every week

Every trade card has room for the pair, the session, the account (live, demo or prop), pips, pip value, spread, slippage, partial closes and swap, with a Wed ×3 tick box for the triple swap. Times go in GMT or server time, so your notes line up with your platform.

Three boxes on every card are framed in bold: Result R, Followed my rules, and Mistake #. They take about twenty seconds and give you the numbers that matter at the weekly review. The trades in “See a filled page” are the sample printed in the book.

Why paper, when your platform already keeps the numbers?

It does keep the numbers. This journal keeps what it can't: the plan before the entry, the rules you followed or broke, how you felt, and the lesson you carry into next week.
