Every leg, every Greek, every roll on record

Each card has a four-leg table, so a vertical spread, a strangle or an iron condor fits on one card. Tick the strategy, note IV and IV rank, the expected move, the position Greeks with their units, probability of profit, buying power and a quick payoff sketch.

Then keep turning: the roll log and the wheel tracker follow a position through assignment, covered calls and the adjusted cost basis at every step. The trades in “See a filled page” are the sample printed in the book.

Why paper, when your broker already keeps the numbers?

It does keep the numbers. This journal keeps what it can't: the plan before the entry, the rules you followed or broke, how you felt, and the lesson you carry into next week.
