The puzzles are the investigation

Thistlewick Green, October 1926. Silas Croft collected rents for nineteen years and made no friends at all. On a wet night he is found at the foot of the mill stairs, his lantern still burning beside him. Twelve villagers had reason enough. Only one was there.

Every grid hides a sentence in its leftover letters. Cross out the words, read what remains, and a witness statement appears. Four grids make one chapter, and each chapter clears exactly one suspect. The whole first chapter is here to solve.
