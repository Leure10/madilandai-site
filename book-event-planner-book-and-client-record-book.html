For the planner, not the couple

This is a working book for a planning business: your calendar, your clients and your vendors. There is no guest list, seating chart or single-wedding budget inside.

Forty-eight quarter-hour rows per day, 8 AM to 8 PM, all seven days the same width, because Saturday and Sunday are when the weddings happen. Write the event of the day under the date, then book the day around it. Undated, so it starts whenever your season starts. The names in “See it filled in” are made up.

Weeks
52 weekly spreads, 48 fifteen-minute slots per day, 8 AM to 8 PM
Every day
An event line under the date and a follow-up line; every week a column for payments and vendor calls, and a line for deposits
Vendors
Preferred vendors on page five: venue, catering, photographer, florist, DJ or band, cake and more, plus a box for your packages and prices
Couples
130 records, A to Z: couple, wedding date, venue, phone or email, package and status
Months
Twelve monthly sections: consultations, bookings, events, deposits and totals
Dates
Undated, start any week
Size
8.5 × 11 in, 122 pages, white paper
