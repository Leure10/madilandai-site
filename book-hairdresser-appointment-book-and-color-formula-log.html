The formula that worked, written down

Page five is a color formula and patch test log with eighteen rows: the date, the client, the formula with brand, shade and developer, the processing time and the patch test. When she comes back, the mix is already there, next to the date of her allergy test.

Around it, 52 weeks of fifteen-minute slots, because a toner starts at 10:15 and a balayage runs past lunch. Undated, so it starts whenever the chair is ready. The names in “See it filled in” are made up.
