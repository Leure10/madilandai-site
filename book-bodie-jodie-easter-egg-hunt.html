A story on the left, its picture on the right

Best friends Bodie and Jodie are helping their neighbor Mrs. Hazel get ready for her Spring Egg Hunt. They meet Clover, a fluffy white bunny, paint eggs (Jodie paints super fast, Bodie paints slow and careful), and decorate the big Sparkle Egg, the egg that is always found last.

On the day of the hunt, Jodie wants to find a hundred eggs. But when little Leo can't find a single one, Bodie and Jodie decide to be his helpers. And when Clover goes missing in the middle of the hunt, everyone learns that the quietest, most patient searchers can find the most wonderful things.

Read together, then color the scene

Younger children listen and color; early readers can read the story on their own. The line art is simple, with big shapes for small hands. A cozy spring adventure with egg painting, a garden egg hunt, a picnic, and seeds planted in eggshells.

What it gently shows

  • Patience, from a shy little bunny to a seed that takes its time to sprout
  • Helping a younger friend find his own way, instead of doing it for him
  • Being gentle with animals and with nature
  • Friendship, sharing, and a lot of giggles
