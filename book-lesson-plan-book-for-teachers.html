A plan book that follows your school year

Most lesson plan books are dated from August to June, so a January start or a summer term wastes half the book. This one is undated: 52 weekly spreads, each running Monday to Friday across eight periods, with a box for every lesson.

Write the subject and time once for each of the eight periods, then plan the lesson in the box beside it. Next to Friday there is room for the week’s to-do list, copies to make, meetings and parent follow-ups. The names in “See it filled in” are made up.
