Initials only, by design

Built for psychologists, therapists, counselors, LMFTs, LCSWs and life coaches who juggle a full caseload every single day.

The Session Log tracks initials, session type, time, focus and next appointment. It uses client initials only, never full names or clinical notes, so you stay privacy-first while keeping your day at a glance. The names in “See a filled week” are made up.
