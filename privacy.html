What we know about you, and what we do not

What this site knows about you, which is very little, and what your rights are.

The short version

This site has no accounts, no advertising, no visitor statistics, no tracking and no cookies. What you color or write in a book here stays in your own browser. We never see it.

Who is responsible

Madiland AI Books
hello@madilandai.com

When you open a page

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (United States). As with any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when your browser asks for a page. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. We do not receive those logs and cannot look anyone up in them.

Our reason for using a host is our legitimate interest in showing you the site and keeping it safe (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub may process this data in the United States; its privacy statement describes the safeguards and how long it keeps the data.

In your browser

What the site stores on your device, why and for how long is listed on Cookies and storage. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. Your time zone and language are read by the page to preselect an Amazon store; they are not stored or sent.

When you go to Amazon

Links to Amazon open in a new tab. From there on, Amazon’s own privacy notice applies. The links carry no affiliate code and we are not told who clicked. Amazon reports sales to us as totals per book, without names or addresses.

If you write to us

We use your message and address to answer you, and delete the exchange when it is no longer needed, at the latest after two years, unless the law requires longer. The basis is our legitimate interest in answering you.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its use, receive it in a portable form, and withdraw a consent at any time without affecting what happened before. Write to the address above; we answer within one month.

You may also complain to a data protection authority. Ours is the Romanian one, ANSPDCP; you may use the one where you live.

We make no automated decisions about anyone. You are never required to give us data.

Children

The coloring pages can be used without giving any information. We do not ask children for anything.

Changes

Last changed on 27 November 2026. If the site starts doing something new with data, this page changes first.
