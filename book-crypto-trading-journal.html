Every entry, every fee, every position sized first

Every trade is logged in UTC, spot or perpetual, with leverage, isolated or cross margin, account equity, and your liquidation price next to your stop, so you see the distance between them before you enter.

Scale-ins get an average price, funding gets its own line, and maker and taker fees are split on entry and exit. It is a record-keeping journal, not financial or tax advice. The trades in “See a filled page” are the sample printed in the book.

Why paper, when your exchange already keeps the numbers?

It does keep the numbers. This journal keeps what it can't: the plan before the entry, the rules you followed or broke, how you felt, and the lesson you carry into next week.

  • Your trading plan and risk rules
  • Mistake codes #1–12 and emotion codes E1–E8
