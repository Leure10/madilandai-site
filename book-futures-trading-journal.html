Every tick, every rule, every day of the evaluation

One page per trading day. The Account & Risk box at the top keeps your prop firm numbers in view before the first trade: starting balance, loss floor and the distance to it, daily loss limit, target left, max contracts and your flat-by time.

Short on time? Three shaded columns, R, Rules and Mistake, are enough for every statistic in the book. At the close, fill the two boxes with a bold frame: Total R and Daily limit respected. The trades in “See a filled page” are the sample printed in the book.
