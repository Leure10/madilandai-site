One picture, one kind word, again and again

A ballerina bunny, a princess with a unicorn friend, a fairy, a tea party, flowers, butterflies and lots of patterned eggs, all drawn in a sweet, simple style that small hands can finish proudly.

On the page facing each picture there is a kind word, set in big friendly letters inside a border that can be colored too. Little ones trace it while a grown-up reads it aloud.
