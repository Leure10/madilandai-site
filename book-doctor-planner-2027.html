Every encounter, in one place

Built for physicians, residents, interns, and medical students. Part daily planner, part rounds organizer, part encounter tracker.

The Patient Encounters table records the time, the patient (use initials to protect privacy), the diagnosis or notes, the Rx or plan, and a follow-up marker, up to five encounters per page. The names in “See a filled week” are made up.
