For the years when the answers get shorter

There is an age when the answers get shorter. Fine. Nothing. I don’t know. It is not that he has stopped having things to say. Saying them out loud, to your face, has become harder than it used to be, and writing them down is easier.

Every question appears twice, on facing pages. Your side on the left, his on the right. You each answer on your own page, then swap. Leave it on his bed. He leaves it on your chair. The answers on the left here are made up.

It starts easy on purpose

Top three meals, in order, no changing your mind later. Only later does it ask what the bravest thing you have ever done was, what you failed at and tried again anyway, and what he needs help with right now.
