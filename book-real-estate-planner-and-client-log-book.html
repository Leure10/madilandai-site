The weekend is when buyers are free

Real estate does not run on the hour. This planner has forty-eight fifteen-minute slots per day, 8:00 in the morning to 8:00 at night, with Saturday and Sunday as full columns.

Each day closes with a miles line instead of a cash total, so the driving between showings is written down the day you drive it. Undated: start it the week you join a brokerage. The names in “See it filled in” are made up.

Weeks
52 weekly spreads, 48 fifteen-minute slots per day, 8 AM to 8 PM
Every day
A miles line; every week a miles total and a column for leads and follow-ups
Listings
One page with 18 rows: address, list price, date listed, status, closing date and commission
Clients
130 records, A to Z: name, phone, buy or sell, price and area, follow-up notes
Goals
Listings, closings and a commission target for each quarter, next to the actual
