Nora Vance & Sam Rourke Mysteries, book 1

Season to Kill

Ten countries. Ten sauces. Ten suspects. One killer with impeccable taste.

The Emperor of Flavor is dead. Augustus Thorne, the critic who could make or break any chef with a single word, collapses at his own New Orleans gala, poisoned by a gift jar of sauce sent by one of the ten disciples he made famous and quietly destroyed.

The police smell cyanide. Nora Vance, a former star chef Gus once ruined, smells something else: beneath the poison, a flavor with a name and an address. To unmask the killer she chases that taste around the globe, with Sam Rourke, the charming, disaster-prone sommelier who loved her once.

From a Veracruz chili oil to a Catalan romesco, from a Marrakech riad to a mist-wrapped Georgian mountain, every kitchen hides a wound and every recipe hides a motive.

Includes 10 real, cookable sauce recipes, one from every country Nora visits.
