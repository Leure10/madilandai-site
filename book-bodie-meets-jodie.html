A story on the left, its picture on the right

One sunny morning, a big moving truck rumbles down Maple Street, and a girl with two wild pigtails cartwheels across the lawn next door. Bodie loves quiet, crayons, and his sketchbook, so he hides behind the sketchbook. The next day in Sunnyside Park, the cartwheel girl and her puppy find him under the old oak tree.

Her name is Jodie, the puppy is Waffles, and the day is about to get very busy: a potato-shaped butterfly to draw, ducks that only come back if you sit as still as stones, a sandcastle that will not stay up, and then a puppy who runs off into a giant sunflower garden. To bring him home, the loud girl and the quiet boy will need each other.

Read together, then color the scene

Younger children listen and color; early readers can read the story on their own. The line art is simple, with big shapes for small hands. A warm choice for bedtime reading and for any child who feels shy around new friends.

What it gently shows

  • How to make a new friend, even when you feel shy
  • That courage can be quiet: “Brave knees can wobble.”
  • That listening is a strength too, and that friends help each other
  • Kindness and sharing, from half an apple to a favorite drawing
