It works because nobody has to make eye contact

You hand it over with your half filled in. She finds it on her bed, reads your answer, writes her own, and leaves it somewhere you will find it. That is the whole mechanism.

Every question appears twice, on facing pages. You answer the same question separately, then read each other. No reading ahead, because waiting for the answer is half the point. The answers on the left here are made up.
