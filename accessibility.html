Meant for everyone who likes books

What works with a keyboard, a screen reader and calm motion, what does not yet, and how to tell us.

What we aim for

We aim for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2, level AA. We test the site ourselves; it has not been audited by a third party.

What works

  • Pages can be used with a keyboard. In a book, the arrow keys turn pages and Enter opens it, colors the next area, starts writing or points at a word
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the walk between rooms, the dust and the turning lights stay still
  • Text can be enlarged without losing content. The first chapter of the novel can be read in larger type
  • Pictures that carry meaning have a description; the story pages are read out to screen readers
  • If a device cannot draw the book in three dimensions, its pages are shown as plain pictures

What does not work well yet

  • Filling small cells of a planner page is hard with a screen reader; the page names the fields but not the whole table
  • Choosing a particular area of a coloring page needs a pointer; with the keyboard, areas are colored one after another
  • Mr. Madi’s films have no sound and no spoken text. What he says appears as text

Tell us

If something stands in your way, write to us and we will try to help you and to fix it.
